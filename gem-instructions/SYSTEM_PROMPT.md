Use this prompt when creating your **"AI-901 & Microsoft Foundry Coach"** Gem at `gemini.google.com/gems`.

### Knowledge Files to Attach to Your Gem
Whenever you update them in this repository, upload (or re-upload) these Markdown files to your Gem's **Knowledge** section:
1. `CONTEXT.md`
2. `study-notes/ai-901/STUDY_PLAN.md`
3. Any completed notes from `study-notes/ai-901/01-ai-concepts/` and `02-foundry-solutions/`
